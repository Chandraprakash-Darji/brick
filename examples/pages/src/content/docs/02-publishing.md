title: Publishing
description: Making pages public and sharing them
---

## Public vs Private

Pages are private by default. Only you can view them when signed in.

Toggle **Public** to make a page visible to anyone with the URL. The slug-based URL is shared automatically.

## Slug

Each page has a unique slug that forms its public URL. Slugs are auto-generated from the title but can be edited manually. They must be unique.

## Updating a page

Click any page in the dashboard to edit it. Changes to public pages go live immediately after saving.
